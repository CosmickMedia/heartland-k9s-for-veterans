Are You Ready

With this initiative, our goal is to promote great opportunities for those in need. With access to the right resources, people can become empowered by their own abilities and gain the confidence to fulfill their potential. Complete the application and submit it to Heartland K9s. Please understand, this is not the application to apply but an opportunity to “see who you are”. Our director will contact you to provide our full application to be considered for our program.
