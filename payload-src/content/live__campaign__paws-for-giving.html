“Paws” for Giving is designed to allow companies, businesses and individuals to provide wall space, counter space or window space for HK9 to put up our specially designed logo with a QR code to give employees or patrons an opportunity to donate to HK9.

Put it on your checkout counter, front display windows, break room wall, car window, anywhere you believe people will see it and help HK9 provide service dogs COST FREE to our Veterans.

The stickers are 4"x4". They are no cost to your business, but gifts are ALWAYS accepted.
