What makes this book special?

This isn’t just a coloring book – it’s a mission in every kid’s hand.

Each page features REAL Heartland service dogs working side-by-side with their Veterans. Kids learn why these dogs matter, how they help with PTSD, mobility, anxiety, night terrors, and more- all in a fun, positive, family-friendly way.

Every copy shared in our community becomes a way to:

  • Teach children about service dogs
  • Honor Veterans and their families
  • Shine a spotlight on businesses that support the mission

This book is designed with love, built with purpose and created to make an impact long after the crayons are put away.
