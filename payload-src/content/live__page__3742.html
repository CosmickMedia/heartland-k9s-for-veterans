Heartland Canines have been providing highly trained service dogs to qualified Veterans for ten years. Not everyone needs a SERVICE dog, but we believe everyone needs a dog. With that, comes a lot of responsibility. Every dog should:

  • Be polite
  • Listen to commands
  • Sit/Lay
  • Heel

All of our training for your dog (and YOU) is possible through Hk9:

  • Multiple Training options
  • All costs (donations) go directly to the training of future service dogs for our Veterans.
  • Flexibility. You come to us, or we come to you.
  • Become part of our family. We do have options available for you and your K9 to become members of our Therapy Dog Program

HK9 owes it to our Veterans and donors that the K9s we receive for the program are trainable, documented, and healthy. The current cost to train and place a K9 in our program is $18,000. We must do everything we can to ensure the K9s in our program are the most likely to be successful in such an important role that is designated to improve the quality of life for our Veterans for as long as possible. None of that is possible without proper fundraising. Your dog gets trained, that cost goes to our program.

Not everyone needs a Service Dog, everyone needs a dog.

Are you looking for a dog, but a service dog isn’t the right fit? That’s ok. Check out our friends over at A Dog’s Dream.

A Dog’s Dream is a compassionate and dedicated dog adoption agency. We believe that every dog’s dream can become a reality. As a pioneering 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, we are dedicated to giving retired breeding dogs a new chapter in life. We achieve this through collaboration with responsible, certified breeders and by providing essential care, enrichment, and training. Our mission is to facilitate the journey of these dogs into loving homes, completing the cycle of compassionate and responsible breeding and adoption. We strive to create a nurturing environment where lasting bonds can flourish between families and their dogs.

They are comprised of a team of passionate individuals who are committed to the welfare of every dog that comes through their doors. They work tirelessly to match each dog with a loving and suitable adoptive family, taking into consideration their unique needs, preferences, and compatibility. Their adoption process involves thorough screening, including veterinary checks and meet & greet visits, to ensure that each dog finds a safe and caring environment for their retirement years.

A Dog’s Dream believes that every retired breeding dog deserves a peaceful and loving retirement. They are dedicated to completing the circle and finding them the perfect fur-ever homes where they can relax, be cherished, and receive the love and care they deserve. If you are ready to open your heart and home to a new dog, we invite you to explore their adoption process and meet the wonderful dogs waiting for their well-deserved retirement.
